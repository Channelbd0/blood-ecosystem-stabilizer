# 🩸 THE BLOOD ECOSYSTEM STABILIZER (BES) 

Welcome to the official repository for the Blood Ecosystem Stabilizer (BES) project. 

If you are here, you believe that life-saving critical care should be a universal right, not a corporate commodity. The BES is an open-source, non-toxic, purely mechanical medical device engineered to maintain the human blood ecosystem during catastrophic failures: **Sickle Cell Crises, Severe Malaria, and Septic Shock**.

By joining this repository, you agree to our Manifesto of Independence. This project operates under a CERN Open Hardware License (Strong Reciprocity). You are free to view, build, and modify this technology, but you are legally barred from patenting it or restricting others from using your modifications. We are completely self-funded by the public, for the public.

## 🗺️ Choose Your Engineering Track

To get involved, look at our Phase 1 Roadmap and assign yourself to a specific track by opening an Issue or introducing yourself on the discussion board.

### 📐 Track A: Hardware & Microfluidics
*   **The Mission:** Designing the mechanical infrastructure and single-use fluid loops.
*   **Current Focus:** 
    *   Drafting 3D CAD models for the Portable Field Unit casing.
    *   Designing the internal microfluidic channels for the single-use diagnostic cartridge.
    *   Selecting medical-grade off-the-shelf peristaltic pump specifications for testing.

### 💻 Track B: Software & Sensor Firmware
*   **The Mission:** Building the 60-second bedside diagnostic "brain."
*   **Current Focus:**
    *   Coding the simulation logic for the Point-of-Care (POC) blood-gas style readings.
    *   Writing firmware to map sensor data (pH, lactic acid, hemoglobin spikes) to automated pump-control triggers.

### 🔋 Track C: Power & Infrastructure
*   **The Mission:** Ensuring the machine can thrive anywhere on Earth using natural sunlight.
*   **Current Focus:**
    *   Designing the power management system for hot-swappable lithium battery packs.
    *   Specifying the folding solar canvas input configurations (ensuring grid independence for rural deployment).

### 📣 Track D: Community & Crowdfunding
*   **The Mission:** Funding the project through the public to maintain absolute independence.
*   **Current Focus:**
    *   Organizing the documentation and building visual assets from Track A's CAD files.
    *   Preparing materials for our upcoming public Equity Crowdfunding Campaign.
